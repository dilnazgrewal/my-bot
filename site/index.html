How buying works

We sell wholesale only and do not sell single pieces to the public.

Minimum order
100 pieces per design, which can be mixed across sizes and colours.
Prices
Prices depend on design, fabric and quantity. The team sends a price list and quote on WhatsApp within one working day.
Delivery times
Ready stock dispatches in 3 to 5 working days. Made-to-order designs take 15 to 25 days.
Shipping
By transport company to anywhere in India. Freight is paid by the buyer.
Payment
50% advance, balance before dispatch, by bank transfer or UPI. No credit for new buyers.
Samples
Up to 5 sample pieces sent by courier. The buyer pays the sample and courier cost.
Private label
The buyer's own brand tag, available from 500 pieces per design.
